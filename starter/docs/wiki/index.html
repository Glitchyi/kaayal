Kaayal, in one page

Kaayal is a voice-first service that connects callers in Kerala — Malayalam or English — to verified boat operators in Alappuzha. A caller dials a phone number and talks to Paayal, a Sarvam-hosted voice agent. Paayal answers general questions, looks up real boat listings, and takes consented booking requests that operators confirm by phone.

The product is deliberately narrow: no payments, no confirmed reservations, no invented prices. Kaayal only ever says what its tools return from real data, and a human operator always closes the loop.

The sixty-second version

  1. A caller dials the Kaayal number. Sarvam owns the call: speech-to-text, a hosted LLM running the Paayal prompt, text-to-speech.
  2. When the caller needs something concrete, Paayal calls one of five HTTP tools on our FastAPI backend over public HTTPS.
  3. The backend either asks OpenAI to draft a short spoken answer (voice_reply), or reads/writes the SQLite catalog directly (boats, booking requests, callbacks).
  4. Operators see their requests on a private portal link; the owner runs everything from /admin. A background callback engine re-contacts consenting callers when availability changes (simulated until outbound calls are approved).
