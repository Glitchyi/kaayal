# Kaayal Marketing Actions

## 0. Ground rules

- **Brand relationship.** *Kaayal* is the company. *Paayal* is its AI voice assistant. Write "Call Paayal" and "Kaayal never confirms prices", never "Paayal books boats".
- **Say only what is true today.** Paayal answers questions in Malayalam and English. Consent-based, availability-triggered callbacks are **planned, not live**. Nothing is booked until an operator says yes [S67].
- **Never:** "first", "only", "best", "verified" or "government approved" without an authoritative record; fake testimonials; invented counts; promised booking volumes.
- **Prerequisites (owner decisions):** call-recording and consent wording (Q02), a native Malayalam reviewer (Q03), and a check that the website's present-tense "request" wording matches what is live (see Section 5).
- **Design system:** unchanged. Use the approved palette and fonts (Fraunces, Satoshi, Noto Sans Malayalam) for every creative.

### A. Ask Paayal (short call videos)

- **Concept.** A 15-25 second screen-recorded call. A Malayalam question appears on screen, Paayal answers, and the end card shows the number. The sample exchange is on slide 7: "what should I check when booking a houseboat?" The point is to hear it work, not to be told.
- **Copy (EN).** "Booking a houseboat? Ask Paayal first. In Malayalam. +91 79658 61153". Caption: "Prototype. Paayal answers questions; the operator confirms price and availability."
- **Copy (ML, draft).** "ഹൗസ്ബോട്ട് ബുക്ക് ചെയ്യും മുൻപ് Paayal-നോട് ചോദിക്കൂ." (Ask Paayal before booking a houseboat.)
- **Channels.** Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp status and shares, Kerala travel Facebook groups (follow each group's rules; disclose you built it).
- **KPIs.** Calls started in the 48 hours after each post (from call logs, if the voice platform provides them); video completion rate; shares and saves.
- **Risks.** A wrong answer goes public. Audit every transcript for invented prices or availability before posting, and get consent from anyone whose voice is recorded.

### B. Before You Book (bilingual checklist plus guide)

- **Concept.** A one-page, shareable "5 questions to ask any houseboat operator", plus a longer web guide. Questions: (1) boat registration; (2) Kerala Tourism classification (Silver/Gold/Diamond) [S08]; (3) what is included (meals, A/C hours, cruise timings); (4) price basis (weekday or peak, taxes, cancellation) [S13][S17]; (5) photos of the actual boat you will get [S06]. CTA: "Ask Paayal".
- **Copy (EN).** "Five questions to ask before you book any Alappuzha houseboat."
- **Copy (ML, draft).** "ഏത് ഹൗസ്ബോട്ട് ഓപ്പറേറ്ററോടും ചോദിക്കേണ്ട 5 കാര്യങ്ങൾ."
- **Channels.** Website guide page, WhatsApp-shareable image, helpful answers on Quora and Reddit (follow the rules, disclose affiliation, link only where allowed).
- **KPIs.** Search Console clicks on the guide; guide-to-call-button taps; checklist shares.
- **Risks.** Legal and safety items must match official wording before they are stated as rules (check against DTPC and Kerala Tourism). SEO results take weeks.

### C. List Once (owner-led operator onboarding)

- **Concept.** A one-page explainer and a 60-second Malayalam voice note from the owner, sent through people the owner already knows, the association, and in person. It uses the WhatsApp contact already on the website [S67].
- **Copy (EN, voice-note script).** "Namaskaram, I'm [owner name] from Kaayal. We are building a Malayalam phone guide for visitors to Alappuzha. If you run boats, you can list them with us once. We check each listing before callers hear about it. As we build the callback feature, requests will reach you only with the caller's consent, and you always confirm price and availability. Nothing is booked until you say yes. If you'd like to talk, message us on WhatsApp."
- **Copy (ML, draft).** To be written by the native reviewer from the script above; do not machine-translate for operators.
- **Channels.** Owner-introduced WhatsApp contacts, the All Kerala Houseboat Owners and Operators Samiti, DTPC introductions, jetty-side conversations. Human messages only; no WhatsApp automation or mass sending (not approved).
- **KPIs.** Conversations held; operators who agree to list; listings the owner verifies. Target 8-10 conversations in the first cycle (a planning goal, not a finding).
- **Risks.** Operators face tax and enforcement pressure [S07][S31], so do not cold-message at scale or imply scrutiny. Do not promise bookings. Do not call any operator "government verified" (the official list we fetched had blank names [S10]).

## 2. Three website content and SEO experiments

None of these changes the design system. Each is a new page or a copy change for Devin, and each needs a baseline before any target is set. Keyword volumes were not measured, so no numeric targets are given here.

| | Experiment | Hypothesis | Measure | Run time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **English trust guide**: "What to ask before you book an Alappuzha houseboat" | Competitors dominate "price" and "book now" queries [S14-S27]; how-to and trust queries are less contested and fit Paayal | Search Console impressions and clicks for 5 chosen queries; guide-to-call taps | 8 weeks |
| **2** | **Malayalam term test**: one Malayalam guide, with "പുരവഞ്ചി" and "ഹൗസ്ബോട്ട്" each in separate headings | Visitors may search either term; "puravanchi" is the official Malayalam word [S56], but "houseboat" may be searched more | Which term earns more impressions and clicks; compare after the baseline | 8 weeks (after native review) |
| **3** | **Official-route explainer**: how the DTPC pre-paid counter works, with FAQ markup | Navigational queries about the official counter [S09][S55] signal trust-seekers, a natural fit for neutral guidance | Impressions and clicks; call taps from this page | 6 weeks |

**Common setup.** One H1 per page; titles up to 60 characters and metas up to 155 (examples in tab `09_Website_Content`); `hreflang` for en/ml; FAQ markup only for approved answers; no review or rating markup. Track call-button taps as an event in privacy-friendly analytics. Verify all official details with DTPC before publishing.

**Success rule.** Before launch, record a 2-week baseline in Search Console. Then compare each experiment with its own baseline and keep or drop it. Do not claim a result from fewer than a few weeks of data.

## 3. Suggested sequence (about 6 weeks)

1. **Week 0 (owner).** Approve the guardrails; answer Q02 and Q03; confirm the site wording (Section 5).
2. **Weeks 1-2.** Build Campaign B and SEO experiment 1; start the Search Console baseline.
3. **Weeks 2-5.** Run Campaign A. Add experiment 3.
4. **Weeks 3-6.** Run Campaign C after the owner approves the script. Add experiment 2 once the Malayalam is reviewed.

## 4. Reporting

One page every two weeks: calls started, language split (Malayalam vs English), share of audited calls with no invented price or availability (target 100%), guide clicks, operator conversations, spend against the Rs 5,000-23,000 assumption. Re-verify any statistic older than 90 days before public use.

## 5. Reconciliation notes (read first)

1. **Site wording vs. planned callbacks.** The live site describes, in present tense, Paayal "describing verified boats" and callers leaving name, number, date and group size for the operator to call back [S67]. You asked that callbacks be presented as a planned capability. The deck and this document do so. Please confirm the website wording matches what is actually live.
2. **Earlier documents.** Our earlier strategy, spec and handoff were written before the site was found. They used the tagline "Alappuzha, in your own language." and "no fees" operator messaging. The site's headline is "The backwaters, a phone call away.", and it states no fees. Use the site's wording. A note at the top of each earlier document says so.
3. **Photography.** None used: no authentic, legally usable Alappuzha photos could be retrieved here, and the site's readable markup showed none. Use the owner's own photography, or Kerala Tourism / DTPC media with written permission.
4. **Site styling.** I could read the site's text and metadata, not its stylesheet. The deck matches the specified fonts, palette and observed structure (single column, hairline rules, numbered steps). Compare it with the live site before sign-off.
